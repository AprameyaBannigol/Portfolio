Course Reflection

Our learning journey in Design and Analysis of Algorithms

Before the Course

At the beginning of the semester, we viewed algorithms mainly as code implementations without deeply considering efficiency or scalability.

What We Learned

We gained a strong understanding of time and space complexity, algorithm design techniques such as divide and conquer, greedy methods, and dynamic programming.

Challenges We Faced

Initially, we struggled to convert problem statements into algorithmic steps and to decide between multiple design approaches.

How We Grew

Over time, our problem-solving approach became more structured. We learned to analyze constraints first and then select the most suitable algorithm.

Reflection on Our Project

Implementing the nearest cab detection system helped us understand how theoretical concepts translate into real-world applications.

Looking Ahead

This course has prepared us for technical interviews and future projects. We plan to continue practicing algorithmic problem-solving.
