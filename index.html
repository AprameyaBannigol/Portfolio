Our Project Timeline

Phase 1: Research & Planning

We began by exploring real-world problems that could benefit from algorithmic solutions. After extensive research, we identified ride-sharing cab detection as our focus area.

Phase 2: Algorithm Selection

We evaluated 12 different algorithms including Dijkstra's, A*, Bellman-Ford, and others. Through comparative analysis, we selected Dijkstra's algorithm for its efficiency in shortest path computation.

Phase 3: Implementation

We developed a complete C++ implementation of our nearest cab detection system. The implementation included graph representation, priority queue optimization, and comprehensive testing.

Phase 4: Testing & Analysis

We conducted thorough complexity analysis, tested with various input scenarios, and validated our algorithm's performance against theoretical expectations.

Phase 5: Documentation & Presentation

We created comprehensive documentation, prepared our final presentation, and built this portfolio website to showcase our work and learning journey.
